## Week 13 Discovery

The project plan remains here:

[Hill Country Coffee is an independent regional coffee company based in Central Texas. It offers espresso drinks, brewed coffee, cold beverages, teas, and a rotating selection of café food. The client needs a modern, responsive website because its current online information has become inconsistent, difficult to scan on mobile devices, and harder for customers to navigate. The site will serve local regulars, people looking for a café while out in the area, visitors exploring the Texas Hill Country, and customers reviewing offerings before visiting. The central solution would be to make the business, menu, and visit information clear and easy to find while communicating the company's welcoming, local character. The project will prioritize mobile usability, understandable navigation, accurate approved content, accessibility, and a manageable course-scale implementation.](docs/discovery-plan.md)
