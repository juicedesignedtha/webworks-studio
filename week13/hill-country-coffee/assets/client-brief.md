# Hill Country Coffee — Client Brief

## Client Overview

Hill Country Coffee is an independent regional coffee company based in Central Texas.

The company wants its website to reflect the relaxed, welcoming character of a Hill Country coffee shop while making practical customer information easy to find.

The current web presence has grown inconsistently over time. Important information is difficult to scan on a phone, visual presentation is inconsistent, and customers sometimes have trouble finding basic information quickly.

WebWorks Studio has been asked to plan and produce a modern, responsive website.

**Week 13 focuses on planning the project.** Interface production begins in Week 14; do not turn the client's brand direction into wireframes or visual comps during this sprint.

---

## Project Goals

The client has identified several concerns. Your job is to analyze and prioritize them rather than simply copying this list into the discovery plan.

The new site should help customers:

- understand what makes Hill Country Coffee distinctive;
- quickly find café/location information;
- explore coffee and food offerings;
- understand the company's connection to local community and the Texas Hill Country;
- find important information easily on mobile devices.

The client also wants a site that feels current and professional without becoming overly complicated to maintain.

---

## Brand Direction

The client describes the desired experience as:

- warm;
- relaxed;
- handcrafted;
- local;
- contemporary;
- welcoming;
- distinctly Central Texas.

The client does **not** want the website to feel:

- corporate;
- luxury/formal;
- rustic in a cliché “cowboy” way;
- cluttered;
- trendy at the expense of usability.

---

## Primary Website Audiences

The client believes the site serves a mix of:

- local regular customers;
- people looking for a coffee shop while out in the area;
- visitors/tourists exploring the Texas Hill Country;
- customers who want to review drinks or food before visiting.

Detailed personas are provided in `personas.md`.

---

## Known Client Priorities

The client has emphasized:

1. Mobile usability is important.
2. Location and hours information should be easy to find.
3. The menu should be easy to scan.
4. The site should communicate the brand without getting in the user's way.
5. Accessibility should be considered throughout the project.
6. The final site should be realistic to complete and maintain.

---

## Project Constraints

- This is a course-scale client project.
- The final website must be achievable within the remaining WebWorks Studio sprints.
- Students should use technologies they can explain and maintain.
- The site must be responsive.
- The site must follow course accessibility standards.
- Source code must be maintained in GitHub.
- Visual Studio Code is required for code editing.
- Do not add complex functionality unless it solves a documented user/client need.
- Do not assume that every technology taught during the semester belongs in the final site.

---

## Client Questions That Are Not Yet Resolved

The client has not supplied:

- online ordering integration;
- e-commerce requirements;
- customer accounts;

- online table reservations;
- a newsletter platform;

If you believe one would be useful, place it in **Future / Out of Scope** unless the instructor later changes the project requirements.

## Success

The client will consider the project successful if customers can quickly understand the business, find practical visit information, explore core offerings, and use the site comfortably across devices.

WebWorks Studio is responsible for translating that broad expectation into testable requirements.
