# Hill Country Coffee — Asset Notes

## Week 13

This folder contains planning source material rather than final production assets.

The final project may later include instructor-supplied:

- logo files;
- coffee/café photography;
- location imagery;
- icons or supporting graphics.

## During Discovery

Record whether an asset is:

- available;
- missing;
- needed for MVP;
- optional.

Do not select random web images and treat them as approved client assets.

## Accessibility Planning

For future imagery, plan for:

- meaningful alternative text when an image communicates content;
- decorative treatment when an image adds no information;
- avoiding text embedded inside images when real HTML text is appropriate;
- adequate contrast when text appears over photography.


## Week 13 Boundary

Use these notes to identify content and asset dependencies. Do not choose final imagery, create visual comps, or design page layouts during Week 13.
