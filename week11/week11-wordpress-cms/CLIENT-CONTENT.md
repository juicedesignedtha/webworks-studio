# Southtown Community Arts — Client Content

## Client Overview

Southtown Community Arts is a nonprofit community arts organization in San Antonio, Texas.

Its mission is to make creative learning, exhibitions, and neighborhood arts programming accessible to people of different ages and experience levels.

The organization wants a small WordPress site that staff members can maintain after launch.

# Required Page: Programs

## Page Title

Programs

## Intro

Creative programs for different interests, schedules, and experience levels.

Programs are designed to be welcoming to beginners while still giving returning participants room to build skills.

## Program 1

### Community Workshops

Short-format workshops introduce participants to a specific medium or technique.

Topics may include:

- Drawing
- Painting
- Printmaking
- Photography
- Mixed media

Most workshops are designed for beginners unless otherwise noted.

## Program 2

### Youth Art Studio

Youth Art Studio offers structured creative sessions for middle-school and high-school students.

Sessions emphasize:

- experimentation;
- creative confidence;
- skill development; and
- respectful studio practice.

## Program 3

### Open Studio Nights

Open Studio Nights provide adults with informal studio time for personal creative work.

Participants bring their own current project or use available prompts for inspiration.

## Reusable Program CTA

### Ready to Join a Program?

Check current availability and upcoming sessions through the organization’s news and announcements.

# Required News Post

## Post Title

Fall Workshop Registration Opens September 3

## Post Content

Registration for Southtown Community Arts fall workshops opens September 3.

This season includes beginner-friendly sessions in drawing, watercolor, printmaking, photography, and mixed media.

Several workshops are offered on evenings and Saturdays.

Space is limited, and registration remains open until each workshop reaches capacity.

### How to Prepare

Before registration opens:

- review the Programs page;
- identify the workshop format that interests you;
- note any age or experience requirements listed in the program description.

Additional fall announcements will be published as schedules are finalized.

# Image Guidance

Use instructor-supplied images if provided in Canvas.

For meaningful images:

- enter concise alternative text that communicates relevant content or purpose;
- do not begin alt text with “image of” or “picture of” unless that wording is genuinely necessary;
- do not use filenames as alt text.

If an image is purely decorative and the editor/theme provides an appropriate decorative treatment, do not invent unnecessary descriptive content.

---

# Content Rules

Do not:

- invent additional programs;
- invent pricing;
- invent staff members;
- invent addresses;
- invent event dates beyond those provided;
- change the organization’s mission or policies.

Your job is to organize and present the supplied content, not rewrite the client into a different organization.
