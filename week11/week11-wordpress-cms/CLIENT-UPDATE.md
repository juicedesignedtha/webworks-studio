# Southtown Community Arts — Client Update Request

## Complete This After the Main Build

Your initial WordPress build has been reviewed. Southtown Community Arts now has one routine content update.

This is a **client-maintainability test**. The goal is to confirm that ordinary content can change without rebuilding the site's global structure.

---

## Client Request

On the **Programs** page, locate the Community Workshops description.

Change this sentence:

> Short-format workshops introduce participants to a specific medium or technique.

To:

> Short-format workshops introduce participants to a focused medium or technique through guided, beginner-friendly practice.

---

## Requirements

1. Make the update through the appropriate WordPress content-editing area.
2. Do **not** rebuild the page layout to make this text change.
3. Do **not** modify the header, footer, primary navigation, or reusable pattern unless the update genuinely requires it. It should not.
4. Preview the Programs page after the change.
5. Confirm that the page still works at desktop and narrow viewport widths.
6. Confirm that the site's shared structure remains consistent.
7. Document the update in the **Client Update Test** section of `README.md`.

---

## What This Tests

A maintainable CMS site should allow routine client content changes without requiring the client to become the web designer.

After the update, you should be able to explain:

- where the editable content lived;
- why that was the correct place to edit it;
- what site-wide structures remained untouched; and
- how the site architecture protected consistency during the update.
