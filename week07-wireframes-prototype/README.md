## 6. Design Rationale
Document approximately three important decisions.

### Decision 1
**Problem: Important difficulty level descriptions are left out/hard to find.**  
**Design response: I added a seprate div that would include only the description of each diffuclty level**  
**Why: This will ensure that each person has a clear understanding of the diffuclty ratings**  

### Decision 2
**Problem: Important trail length information is not clear or easy to understand.**  
**Design response: I added a small portion dedicated to the quick important informant like time of completion and trail length.**  
**Why: Important information should not be lost in big paragraphs or descriptions.**  

### Decision 3
**Problem: A visual depiction of the trails is not viewable.**  
**Design response: I refered the space that was prevously used for color descriptions for the difficulty.**  
**Why: This will be a much better use of this space, and will allow people to compare trails more effeictly**
