# Southtown Community Arts — Week 12 Brand Specification

Use this document as the approved visual direction for the Week 12 customization sprint.

The goal is a **warm, contemporary community-arts identity** that remains readable, welcoming, and easy for staff to maintain.

## Brand Personality
- creative but not chaotic
- welcoming
- local
- contemporary
- approachable
- community-centered

## Approved Palette

| Role | Name | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Primary dark | Southtown Deep | `#17313f` |
| Primary accent | Southtown Teal | `#2b7a70` |
| Warm accent | Southtown Warm | `#c7855a` |
| Soft surface | Southtown Soft | `#eef5f3` |
| Light surface | Southtown Paper | `#ffffff` |

### Palette Use
Use the palette as a system rather than placing every color everywhere.

- **Southtown Deep**: strong text, headings, or dark structural areas.
- **Southtown Teal**: primary interactive/brand accent where contrast is appropriate.
- **Southtown Warm**: secondary accent; use selectively.
- **Southtown Soft**: light supporting surfaces or section backgrounds.
- **Southtown Paper**: primary light surface.

**Accessibility requirement:** Do not assume every brand-color pairing is accessible. Test the actual foreground/background combinations you use.

## Typography Direction

Use typography controls available in the assigned WordPress block theme.

- **Headings:** confident, clear, contemporary sans-serif treatment.
- **Body:** highly readable sans-serif treatment.
- **Hierarchy:** use size, weight, spacing, and placement consistently.
- Avoid decorative or novelty typography that reduces readability.

You are **not** required to install external fonts.

## Interface Direction

The customized site should feel:
- spacious rather than crowded;
- consistent rather than page-by-page;
- clearly branded without excessive decoration;
- easy to scan;
- usable at narrow and wide viewport widths.

## Buttons and Links

- Primary actions should be visually identifiable.
- Hover and focus states must remain visible.
- Do not remove underlines or focus indicators unless an equally clear accessible treatment replaces them.
- Do not rely on color alone to communicate state.

## Reusable Pattern Direction

Your branded reusable pattern should keep a stable structure while allowing editors to replace the content.

A useful pattern may include:
- an eyebrow/category label;
- heading;
- short supporting copy;
- CTA;
- optional image/media area.

The exact layout is your design decision.

## Scope Reminder

Global brand decisions belong in Global Styles or the appropriate site-level system.

Local content stays local.

Reusable structure stays reusable.

Use one small development-level modification only when the available WordPress controls do not reasonably solve a legitimate requirement.
