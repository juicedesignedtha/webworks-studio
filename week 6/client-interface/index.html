Juniper Creek Loop

Juniper Creek Loop follows a seasonal drainage and includes several rocky crossings. Hikers should use caution after rain. The route is popular during cool weather. Parking is available near the western entrance, and visitors should arrive early on weekends. Carry water. Some portions have limited shade.

Trail classification: Easy. Dogs may be allowed depending on current park rules. Fees and gate hours are subject to change.

Painted Bluff Trail

Painted Bluff climbs steadily from the trailhead. Several sections have loose rock, and the upper bluff is exposed. This route can feel strenuous during warm weather. Check park information before arriving.

Live Oak Ridge

Live Oak Ridge is longer than many introductory routes but has relatively gentle grades. The route passes several junctions. Bring water and confirm closing time before starting late in the day.

Before you go

Weather, access, fees, and regulations can change. Check current park information before leaving home.
