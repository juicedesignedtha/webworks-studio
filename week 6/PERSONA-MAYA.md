# User Persona — Maya Torres

## The Cautious Weekend Explorer

**Age:** 32  
**Occupation:** Administrative coordinator  
**Location:** San Antonio, Texas  
**Technology:** Primarily uses her phone; comfortable with everyday apps and websites  
**Hiking experience:** Beginner  
**Fitness level:** Moderately active, but does not regularly hike  
**Typical companions:** A friend or her sister

## About Maya
Maya enjoys being outdoors and wants to hike more often, especially on weekends and while traveling. She has walked paved greenways and easy nature trails, but she has little experience with traditional hiking trails.

She does not always understand hiking terms such as *technical*, *scramble*, *exposure*, or *elevation gain*. She wants somewhere beautiful, some exercise, and a route she can finish confidently.

## Goals
- Find trails appropriate for a beginner.
- Understand how difficult a trail really is.
- Know approximately how long the hike will take.
- See distance and elevation information.
- Know what to bring.
- Understand parking, fees, and trail access before leaving home.
- Quickly determine whether dogs are allowed.

## Behaviors
- Searches on her phone, often the night before.
- Scans pages rather than reading every paragraph.
- Looks at photographs before reading details.
- Compares several trails.
- Reads reviews when difficulty is unclear.
- Shares a trail link with her hiking companion.
- Screenshots important information in case service is poor.

## Frustrations
- Difficulty labels without explanation.
- Important facts buried in long text.
- Information scattered across several pages.
- Hard-to-read mobile layouts.
- Maps that are difficult to interpret on a phone.
- Attractive photography with little practical information.
- Conflicting or vague trail information.

## Accessibility & Usability Needs
Maya does not identify as having a disability, but she benefits from:
- clear headings;
- readable text;
- sufficient contrast;
- descriptive links and buttons;
- information conveyed through text as well as icons/color;
- large touch targets;
- concise, scannable content; and
- plain-language explanations.

## Discussion Quote
> “I want a pretty hike, not a survival story.”
