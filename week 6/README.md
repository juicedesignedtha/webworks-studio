## 2. Five UX Findings

Document **exactly five meaningful findings**.

### Finding 1
**Observation:**  
[The difficulty level is only explained by color, something that is "easy" for a expert is not the same as "easy" to a noivce.]

**Evidence:**  
[Each of the differnt trails are color coded in either (Green/Orange/Red). The diffuclty range is not explaned anywhere else.]

**User Impact:**  
[This could easliy deter Maya from confidently choosing the right trail]

**Principle:**  
[Clarity]

**Priority:** High- understanding they recommened level is important to user saftey.

**Recommendation:**  
[Consider having an indepth explanation of the difficulty rating somewhere in the webpage(easy to locate)]

### Finding 2
**Observation:[ The important information is held on a seprate section, making it diffcult to compare information from trail to trail. ]**  
**Evidence:[ When clicking for more information about each trail, you end up jumping to seperate parts of the page.]**  
**User Impact:[ This will in turn make the process of last minute late night planning, take much longer than needed.]**  
**Principle:[ Cognitive load]**  
**Priority:**  [Moderate -not critical to safety or usabilty, but still an important function]
**Recommendation:** [Make a drop of information or include important details in the bio.] 

### Finding 3
**Observation:[ No directions are given to help find the location of these parks, forcing users to jump back and fourth inbeteween diffirent apps. ]**  
**Evidence:[ There is only names provided of each park rather than a true location.]**  
**User Impact:[ This puts a little more work on Maya, if this web is meant to make the search easier, it should'nt make the search tedious.]**  
**Principle:[ Cognitive load]**  
**Priority:[ Moderate -not critical to safety or usabilty, but still a very useful function]**  
**Recommendation:[ I would recommend finding a way to implicate loction in each box]**  

### Finding 4
**Observation:[ There are no clear explination of time or distantance of each trail. ]**  
**Evidence:[ we are given multiple differnet measurement, all of which are un-explained. (6.1mi 410ft)]**  
**User Impact:[ This makes Maya do her own deep dive into each location, making late night searches not as simple.]**  
**Principle:[Cognitive load/Navigation/Clarity]**  
**Priority:[High- it goes against way to many principles]**  
**Recommendation:[I would recommend a better explanation of the given measurements.]**  

### Finding 5
**Observation:[ No photos are given for each trail, taking away from atraction]**  
**Evidence:[ Each trail has a box above with a color-fill that aligns with its designated diffuclty rating]**  
**User Impact:[ This would make it difficult for Maya to scroll through and use visaul appeal to find a trail that fits her wants.]**  
**Principle:[CLarity/Cognitive load]**  
**Priority:[High- do to primary users prefrences]**  
**Recommendation:[ I would recommend including images for each trail rather then just a discriptive bio]**
