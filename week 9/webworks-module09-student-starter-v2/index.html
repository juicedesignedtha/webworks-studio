Plan Your Visit

Know before you go.

The market is designed for a relaxed Saturday visit. Most guests spend between 60 and 90 minutes browsing.

Parking
Free parking is available in the east lot. Accessible spaces are closest to the main entrance.
Food
Two local food vendors and a coffee cart will be open during market hours.
Payment
Most vendors accept cards and digital payments. Some also accept cash.
Accessibility
The venue has a step-free entrance, accessible restrooms, and seating throughout the hall.

Can I bring children?

Yes. The market is family-friendly. Children should remain with an adult, especially during live demonstrations.

Are pets allowed?

Only trained service animals are allowed inside Mission Plaza Arts Hall.

Can I buy items online later?

Many vendors sell online, but availability varies. Ask the maker for a business card or website before you leave.

Is the event indoors?

Yes. The maker booths and demonstrations are indoors. Food vendors are located on the covered south patio.
