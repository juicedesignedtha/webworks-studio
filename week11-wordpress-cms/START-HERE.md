# Start Here — Week 11

## Your Job

Week 11 is a WordPress/CMS configuration sprint.

You are **not** installing WordPress, building a custom theme, writing PHP, or completing Week 12 customization work.

Your job is to use the instructor-supplied WordPress environment and block theme to organize client content in a maintainable way.

The professional goal is simple:

> Build a site the client can update without rebuilding the site's structure.

---

## Included in This Starter

- `CLIENT-CONTENT.md` — all supplied Southtown Community Arts content
- `CLIENT-UPDATE.md` — the post-build client update you must complete
- `README.md` — your required CMS handoff and evidence template
- `evidence/` — folder for the six required screenshots

---

## Setup and Workflow

1. Create or use this folder inside your semester repository:

```text
webworks-studio/week11-wordpress-cms/
```

2. Keep this starter folder for your documentation and evidence.
3. Open the WordPress site supplied by your instructor.
4. Read `CLIENT-CONTENT.md` before entering content.
5. Complete the primary WordPress build described in Canvas.
6. Read `CLIENT-UPDATE.md` **after** the main build is complete.
7. Complete the supplied client update through the appropriate WordPress editing area.
8. Confirm that the update did not require rebuilding the site's global structure.
9. Test the visitor experience and the client-editing experience.
10. Capture the required screenshots.
11. Save the screenshots in `evidence/` using the exact filenames below.
12. Complete `README.md`.
13. Commit and push the Week 11 handoff folder.
14. Submit the direct Week 11 GitHub folder link in Canvas.

## Important

Do not invent new client facts, programs, dates, policies, or services.

Use the supplied content and organize it appropriately inside WordPress.

Keep Week 11 focused on:

- content vs. structure;
- blocks;
- pages and one supplied post;
- navigation;
- reusable patterns;
- site-wide structure;
- client-editable content;
- responsive/accessibility checks; and
- client maintainability.

Do **not** add plugins, custom CSS, PHP, `theme.json` work, or other Week 12 customization unless your instructor specifically directs you to do so.

Refer to the Canvas assignment for complete requirements.
