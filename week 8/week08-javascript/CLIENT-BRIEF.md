# Hill Country Trail Guide — Client Brief

## Client
Hill Country Trail Guide is a fictional regional trail-finding service for people exploring parks and natural areas in and around South Texas.

## Business Goal
Help visitors confidently choose a trail that fits their experience, available time, and comfort level.

## Primary User for Weeks 6–8
**Maya Torres — The Cautious Weekend Explorer**

Maya is 32, lives in San Antonio, and has limited hiking experience. She usually researches hikes on her phone the night before. She wants a scenic hike and some exercise without accidentally choosing a trail that is too difficult.

### Maya's Core Questions
Before choosing a trail, Maya wants to know:
- Is this trail really appropriate for a beginner?
- How long will it take?
- How far is it?
- How much climbing is involved?
- What does the difficulty label actually mean?
- What should I bring?
- Are there parking fees or access restrictions?
- Are dogs allowed?
- Can I quickly compare choices on my phone?

## Primary Task
**Choose a beginner-appropriate Saturday hike that can be completed in about three hours or less.**

## Three-Week Studio Progression
- **Week 6 — Diagnose:** Evaluate the current interface and prioritize UX problems.
- **Week 7 — Design:** Turn Week 6 findings into requirements, wireframes, and a prototype.
- **Week 8 — Develop:** Implement meaningful browser behavior with vanilla JavaScript.

The client and user remain the same across all three weeks, but the deliverable changes.
