Plain-language help

Not sure what “Easy” or “Moderate” means?

Difficulty guide

Easy: Generally suitable for newer hikers who are comfortable walking on natural surfaces. Distance, heat, and elevation still matter.

Moderate: May include sustained climbing, rougher surfaces, exposed areas, longer distance, or conditions that require more stamina and preparation.

Difficulty labels are only a starting point. Compare distance, estimated time, elevation gain, conditions, and your own experience.
